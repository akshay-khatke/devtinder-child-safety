Child Safety Standards

Last Updated: October 8, 2026

1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

DevTinder is committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users. We have a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

DevTinder does not tolerate the use of our platform for the sexual exploitation, abuse, or endangerment of children.

2. Prohibited Activities

DevTinder strictly prohibits any content or behavior involving the sexual exploitation or abuse of children.

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Creation, upload, sharing, or distribution of CSAM.
  • Sexual exploitation or abuse of children.
  • Sexualization of minors.
  • Grooming or attempts to establish inappropriate sexual relationships with minors.
  • Using DevTinder to facilitate or promote child exploitation.
  • Any other activity that violates applicable child-safety laws.

3. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report child-safety concerns through the reporting functionality available within the DevTinder application.

Users should provide as much relevant information as possible when submitting a report.

Useful information may include:

  • The username or profile involved.
  • The content or behavior being reported.
  • Relevant screenshots or available information.
  • Any additional information that may help our investigation.

Reports involving child safety are treated seriously and may result in immediate enforcement action.

4. Child Safety Contact

For child-safety concerns, including suspected CSAE or CSAM, users, authorities, and other relevant parties can contact our designated child-safety contact.

5. Investigation and Enforcement

DevTinder may investigate reports of suspected child-safety violations and take appropriate action based on the circumstances.

Possible actions include:

  • Removing prohibited content.
  • Suspending or permanently terminating accounts.
  • Restricting access to DevTinder.
  • Preserving relevant information where appropriate.
  • Cooperating with law enforcement and relevant authorities when legally required.

6. Cooperation With Authorities

DevTinder is committed to complying with applicable child-safety laws and regulations.

Where legally required, DevTinder will cooperate with relevant regional and national authorities and provide information in accordance with applicable law and valid legal processes.

7. Protection of Minors

DevTinder does not permit the use of the platform for sexual interactions involving minors.

We encourage users to report suspected child-safety violations immediately through the application or by contacting our designated child-safety contact.

8. Policy Updates

We may update these Child Safety Standards from time to time to improve our safety practices and maintain compliance with applicable laws and platform requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page.
